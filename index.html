About

A Melbourne GP with a long view of her patients' health.

Born and raised in Melbourne, Melanny completed her Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery with Honours at Monash University, followed by residency across metropolitan and regional Victorian hospitals. She holds a fellowship in General Practice with the RACGP and a Diploma in Child Health.
